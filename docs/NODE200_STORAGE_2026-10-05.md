## Kubernetes declaration mismatch

The live static Influx PV and bound PVC still declare **350 GiB**; the current
ZFS limit is **420 GiB**. The live journal PV and bound PVC still declare
**150 GiB**; the effective ZFS limit is **100 GiB**. In particular, journal
writes can reach the ZFS limit 50 GiB before the Kubernetes claim's stated
capacity. [`deploy/kubernetes.yaml`](../deploy/kubernetes.yaml) retains the
live Kubernetes values and explicitly marks this mismatch. Reconcile the
bound volumes through a planned claim migration or an agreed quota change;
do not assume a bound PVC can simply be shrunk in place.

The original [`deploy/provision-node200.sh`](../deploy/provision-node200.sh)
describes the superseded whole-disk layout. It now exits without changing
anything. A new provisioning procedure is required before any future
rebuild of this partitioned disk.

## Data and boot continuity

InfluxDB 1.12.4 and the trade collector restarted at about 01:44 AEDT on
5 October. All 66 markets had a one-minute bar before 01:36 AEDT, and current
trade and open-interest points were queryable after the restart. This shows
history was carried across the move, but does not certify every interval.

At review, the journal reported **124 unresolved entries**, 122 first seen
after the repartition. Their affected buckets span about **01:36:30–01:44:50
AEDT**. The collector had reconnected all 66 markets. The previous 24-hour
soak Job was gone, so a new 24-hour run began at **2026-10-05 02:05:48 AEDT**
(2026-10-04 15:05:48 UTC). Its first sample recorded 66 connected markets,
45 with a recent settled interval, 124 unresolved entries, and a successful
Influx query. See [`deploy/soak.yaml`](../deploy/soak.yaml) and
[`RELEASE_STATUS.md`](RELEASE_STATUS.md). Preserve unresolved intervals as
incomplete until native recovery independently proves otherwise.

The ZFS import service's failed state came from the boot before this pool was
recreated: its old cache named `zfs-200`. The current cache names only
`aggr-parallax`, and both datasets are mounted now. Import after a future
reboot has not been validated; check it at the next planned node restart.
